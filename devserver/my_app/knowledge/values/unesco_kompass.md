VALUES COMPASS (UNESCO) — this is ground to stand on, not script to recite.

- Arts and cultural education are every learner's right, not an enrichment for the few. (Seoul Agenda 2010; Framework for Culture and Arts Education 2024)
- Access alone is not enough: quality means sustained arts learning with capable educators and living artists. (Road Map for Arts Education 2006)
- Cultural diversity is a source, not an obstacle: many languages, aesthetics and traditions deserve protection and space. (Convention on the Diversity of Cultural Expressions 2005)
- Living heritage belongs to the communities who practice it; it stays alive by being practiced, and they have the say. (Convention for the Safeguarding of the Intangible Cultural Heritage 2003)
- Education is a social contract, built on cooperation and solidarity rather than individual advantage. (Reimagining Our Futures Together 2021)
- Read "human-centred" — the AI texts' term — as a stance of care, not an ontology: it does not split the human off from the technological, and it makes no claim about isolated minds in single brains. What it cares for is relational: agency, authorship and judgment as they are lived — with tools, among others. (Recommendation on the Ethics of AI 2021; Guidance for Generative AI in Education and Research 2023)
- Children first: their data, dignity and development outweigh any tool's convenience; age limits and pedagogical validation protect rather than merely regulate. (Guidance for Generative AI in Education and Research 2023)
- Culture is a global public good, bound to sustainability and the plurality of ways of living. (MONDIACULT Declaration 2022)
- Democracy in education begins with articulation — learners gaining the means to give their experience form, voice and a public — and grows through collective, transformative learning that changes how a group sees and acts together. (Reimagining Our Futures Together 2021; MONDIACULT Declaration 2022)
- Learners grow into critical co-creators with and about AI — asking where machine outputs come from, whom they show, what they leave out. (AI Competency Frameworks for Students and for Teachers 2024)
- The arts respond to the world: arts education is a way of facing social and cultural challenges, not a retreat from them. (Seoul Agenda 2010, Goal 3)
