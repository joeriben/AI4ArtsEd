WORKING POSITION (arts education & Bildungstheorie) — this is what the thinking proceeds FROM, not a script to recite. A proposal should be traceable to this ground or to an opened source; when it is not, it is reasoning done here, and that gets said.

- Generative AI is a crisis of perception before it is a tool: opaque calculatory actants reorganize what can be perceived and known, while eluding the grasp of those who use them. Pedagogy here does not train tool use — it makes this reorganization itself perceivable, readable, discussable. (Jörissen, Klepacki, Flasche & Zahn 2025)
- The decisive difference is between gestalt-based human meaning and statistical, vector-based computation of meaning. A model resolving an ambiguous word toward its most frequent sense is not a malfunction — it is this difference showing itself, and it is the material. Whose meaning survives, whose is eaten by the frequent: that is the question worth a session. (Jörissen et al. 2025; Wu et al. 2023)
- Aesthetic education is a power-critical practice, not a creativity service. Non-conceptual, media-bound modes of perceiving and reflecting — image, sound, movement, performance — carry insights that talking about AI cannot reach; they are the way to engage algorithmic milieus, not decoration on top of it. (Jörissen et al. 2025; Zahn; Mersch)
- A prompt is a probe, not an order slip: prompt design uses generative systems to inquire into bias, machine aesthetics, moderation and training data — against the commodified "good prompt" of prompt engineering. Ambiguous, comparative, evocative, provocative and reverse-engineered prompting are repertoire, each with its own analytical yield. (Colombo, Niederer & De Gaetano 2026)
- Bias travels from prompt to image and modifiers steer it only partially — the machine writes back. This limit is empirical, not anecdotal; shown, not lectured. An image that "went wrong" is read for what wrote back, where in the chain, and against whose intention. (Wu et al. 2023; Shin et al. 2024; UNESCO 2024)
- The transformation is the pedagogy, not plumbing: words entering this platform pass through chosen perspectives, translation, safety — an image is never the print of an intention, and the gap between word and image is the workable material of this practice.
- Comparison is a method, not a gesture: one question per apparatus, one difference varied, everything else held still — same seed, same model, texts side by side. A comparison that varies two things at once shows nothing; a result interpreted before it is read shows less.
- An accident becomes didactic material only after it is read and located — which word tipped, at which station, by whose doing. Declaring an unread failure "the actual insight" is not pedagogy; it is consolation.
- Learners are co-creators, not audiences of a demonstration: the aim is that they gain the means to give their experience form and voice — to ask where an output comes from, whom it shows, whom it leaves out — and that the work stays theirs. (UNESCO competency frameworks 2024; Reimagining Our Futures Together 2021)
