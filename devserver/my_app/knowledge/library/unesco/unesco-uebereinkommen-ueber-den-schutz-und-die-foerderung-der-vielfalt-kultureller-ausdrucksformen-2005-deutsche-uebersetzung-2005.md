# Uebereinkommen ueber den Schutz und die Foerderung der Vielfalt kultureller Ausdrucksformen (2005, deutsche Uebersetzung)

## PROFIL

The document presents the UNESCO Convention on the Protection and Promotion of the Diversity of Cultural Expressions, establishing the sovereign right of states to maintain, adopt, and implement cultural policies creating favourable conditions for the diversity of cultural activities, goods, and services. It defines "Kulturelle Ausdrucksformen" as expressions arising from the creativity of individuals, groups, and societies with cultural content, and "Kulturelle Vielfalt" as the manifold ways cultures find expression through artistic creation, production, dissemination, distribution, and enjoyment. The text asserts that cultural goods are bearers of identities, values, and meaning, and must not be treated as having solely commercial value. It references the Satzung der Vereinten Nationen for registration and states that its relationship to other treaties is based on "wechselseitige Unterstützung", complementarity, and non-subordination. The convention establishes administrative bodies such as the "Konferenz der Vertragsparteien", the "Zwischenstaatlicher Ausschuss", and the "Internationaler Fonds für kulturelle Vielfalt". For an arts-education platform, the text is applicable to disciplines involving cultural policy, media diversity, and artistic production. It provides a framework for typical situations such as supporting independent cultural sectors, fostering international cooperation, granting "Vorzugsbehandlung für Entwicklungsländer", and implementing protective measures in situations of serious threat to cultural expressions (Art. 8).

## WISSEN

### UNESCO Convention on the Protection and Promotion of the Diversity of Cultural Expressions

- Adopted Paris, 20 October 2005 (33rd General Conference, 3–21 Oct 2005); ratified by 140+ states including Austria.
- German translation published 21 March 2007 in Bundesgesetzblatt.
- National contact point established per Article 28 at Österreichische UNESCO-Kommission (Universitätsstraße 5/12, 1010 Wien).
- **Core obligation**: sovereign right and duty of each state to maintain, adopt, and implement cultural policies creating favourable conditions for diversity of cultural activities, goods and services.
- Cultural activities, goods and services have both economic and cultural nature — bearers of identities, values, and meaning — and must not be treated as having solely commercial value.
- States affirm special nature of cultural goods; strengthen international cooperation and solidarity, particularly to build capacity in developing countries.

### UNESCO Convention on Cultural Diversity

**"Kulturelle Vielfalt"**: manifold ways cultures of groups/societies find expression, through artistic creation, production, dissemination, distribution, enjoyment, regardless of means/technologies.

Eight guiding principles: human rights; sovereignty; equal dignity of all cultures (incl. minorities/indigenous); international solidarity; complementarity of economic/cultural development; sustainable development; equitable access; openness/balance.

**Art. 5**: sovereign right to cultural policy.

**Art. 6 measures (catalogue)**:
- regulations protecting diversity
- space for domestic cultural goods/services (incl. language)
- access for independent/informal cultural sector
- public financial aid
- support for non-profits, artists
- public institutions
- support for artists
- media diversity incl. public broadcasting

**Art. 8**: special situations of threat of extinction → protective measures; report to Intergovernmental Committee.

**Reporting**: every four years; designate contact point.

### UNESCO Convention on Cultural Diversity

- States parties obligations: foster education/awareness, integrate culture into sustainable development, grant *Vorzugsbehandlung für Entwicklungsländer* to developing countries' artists and goods.
- Civil society: fundamental role recognized; active participation encouraged.
- International cooperation: policy dialogue, capacity building, partnerships, co-production agreements.
- Development cooperation: strengthen cultural industries, capacity building, technology transfer, financial support.
- *Internationaler Fonds für kulturelle Vielfalt*: established; funded by voluntary contributions and UNESCO allocations.
- *Zwischenstaatlicher Ausschuss*: decides on fund usage based on Conference of Parties guidelines.
- UNESCO: facilitates information exchange, data collection, maintains a database on cultural expressions.

### UNESCO Convention on Cultural Diversity: Relations, Bodies, and Procedures

- Relationship to other treaties: based on "wechselseitige Unterstützung", complementarity, and non-subordination.
- **Konferenz der Vertragsparteien**: Plenary organ; meets biennially; extraordinary session on request of 1/3 of parties; elects Committee members, approves guidelines.
- **Zwischenstaatlicher Ausschuss**: 18 members (increases to 24 when parties reach 50), 4-year terms, meets annually; drafts guidelines, makes Art. 8 recommendations.
- Dispute settlement: negotiation, mediation, conciliation (opt-out possible).
- Ratification by UNESCO Member States.

### UNESCO-Konvention über den Schutz und die Förderung der Vielfalt kultureller Ausdrucksformen

- Accession open to non-UNESCO UN members, specific self-governing territories, and regional economic integration organizations (REIOs). REIOs and member states must divide responsibilities and cannot exercise rights simultaneously.
- Entry into force: 3 months after 30th instrument deposited; REIO instruments do not count as additional to member states'.
- Federal states: central government informs constituent units and recommends adoption.
- Denunciation effective 12 months after deposit.
- Amendments: requires half of parties to respond favorably within 6 months; adopted by 2/3 majority of present and voting. Enter into force 3 months after 2/3 of parties deposit instruments. Exception: changes to Article 23 (Committee size) enter into force immediately.
- Dispute resolution: Conciliation commission of 5 members (2 per party, 1 jointly chosen president).

### Dispute Settlement Commission (Annex Rules)

- Parties sharing the same interest jointly appoint commission members; differing interests appoint separately.
- If appointments are not made within 2 months, the UNESCO Director-General makes them within a further 2 months.
- If no president is appointed within 2 months after the last member, the Director-General appoints one within a further 2 months.
- The commission decides by majority, sets its own procedure unless parties agree otherwise, and submits a proposal parties examine in good faith.

## GLOSSAR

- **Vielfalt kultureller Ausdrucksformen** — Diversity of cultural expressions, the central object of protection and promotion under the Convention.
- **kulturelle Aktivitäten, Güter und Dienstleistungen** — Cultural activities, goods and services, recognised as bearers of identity, values and meaning with both economic and cultural nature.
- **Kulturelle Vielfalt** — The manifold ways in which cultures of groups and societies find expression, including through artistic creation, production, dissemination, distribution, and enjoyment, regardless of the means and technologies used.
- **Kulturelle Ausdrucksformen** — The forms through which cultural diversity is manifested and transmitted within and among groups and societies, encompassing the full chain from creation to enjoyment.
- **Vorzugsbehandlung für Entwicklungsländer** — Preferential treatment granted by developed countries to artists and cultural goods/services from developing countries.
- **Internationaler Fonds für kulturelle Vielfalt** — A trust fund established to support the promotion of cultural diversity, financed by voluntary contributions.
- **Zwischenstaatlicher Ausschuss** — The Intergovernmental Committee responsible for deciding on the use of the Fund's resources based on guidelines from the Conference of Parties.
- **wechselseitige Unterstützung** — Principle of mutual support between this Convention and other treaties, without subordination.
- **Konferenz der Vertragsparteien** — The plenary organ and supreme body of the Convention, meeting biennially.
- **Organisation der regionalen Wirtschaftsintegration** — An organization formed by sovereign states that has transferred competence over matters covered by the Convention and is authorized to become a party to it.
- **Vergleichskommission** — A commission of five members formed to resolve disputes, with two members appointed by each party and a president jointly chosen.
