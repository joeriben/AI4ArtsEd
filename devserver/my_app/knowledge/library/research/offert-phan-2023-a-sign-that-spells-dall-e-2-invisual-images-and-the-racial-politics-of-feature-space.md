# A Sign That Spells. DALL·E 2, Invisual Images and The Racial Politics of Feature Space

## PROFIL

Fabian Offert argues that generative machine learning systems like DALL·E 2 produce a new politics of visual culture through "feature extraction" and "semantic compression," which paradoxically reproduce "whiteness" as a "machinic concept" despite being "inhuman, invisual, and opaque." The essay sets itself against popular critiques that merely point out a lack of representation and against corporate "debiasing" techniques that rely on quick technical fixes to mitigate commercial loss. Instead, it contends that current-generation "foundation models" require current-generation humanist critique that identifies the technical modes of whiteness at play, advocating for intervention through "using language rather than having language." For arts education, the text offers a critical framework for understanding and actively manipulating the latent biases of generative image tools via prompt-based strategies.

## WISSEN

### Abstract and Problem Framing

Generative ML systems like DALL·E 2 produce a new politics of visual culture through feature extraction and semantic compression — techniques that are inhuman, invisual, and opaque, yet consistently reproduce whiteness as a latent feature of dominant visual culture. OpenAI's failed 'debiasing' efforts serve as a critical opening to interrogate how such systems dissolve and reconstitute politically salient concepts like race, while anti-racism is reduced to technical fixes mitigating commercial risk.

### DALL·E 2 and the Debiasing Technique

OpenAI released DALL·E 2 in April 2022, shifting public concern from language to image models, foregrounding racial bias. On July 18, 2022, OpenAI claimed a new 'technique' for diverse image generation. Andy Baio's experiment with the prompt 'a sign that spells' revealed the method: DALL·E 2 returned signs reading 'woman', 'Africa', 'black'. The 'debiasing' technique simply appended gendered/racialized keywords to prompts—literally putting words in the user's mouth. It did not fix the model but the user.

### Reconfiguring Visual Culture and Semantic Compression

The shift from model-based to user-based debiasing signals that large visual models are treated as 'complete'—beyond improvement—reflecting the 'foundation model' ideology. Drawing on Amoore, this marks a displacement of rules-based orders by machine learning functions profiting from volatility. Large visual models reconceptualize reality via feature extraction and semantic compression: politically salient concepts like race are 'dissolved' in feature space, then resurrected as ahistorical and apolitical.

<!-- quelle: Reconfiguring Visual Culture and Semantic Compression -->

### The Durability of Whiteness

DALL·E 2's "biased" outputs are not statistical errors but accurate portrayals of whiteness dominating Western visual culture. Whiteness endures through semantic compression into features, yet is rarely named in system cards. OpenAI's blunt fix—hidden randomized keywords—conceals rather than confronts racial injustice, operating as liberal multiculturalism's management of difference.

<!-- quelle: The Durability of Whiteness -->

### Conclusion: New Modes of Humanist Critique

Current machine learning models demand new humanist critique. Pointing out lacks (data, representation) fails when systems are deemed complete reality representations. Critique must identify the *technical modes of whiteness* as a *machinic concept*. Since computing is language-based, critique means *using language*—e.g., weighting prompts, bypassing censorship via misspelling, or using metalanguage—to manipulate the machine.

## GLOSSAR

- **feature extraction** — A cultural technique by which generative ML systems decompose images into machine-readable structural components.
- **invisual** — Describing image-making that operates without human visual perception or interpretation, yet still produces visual-cultural effects.
- **latent feature** — An implicit, statistically derived attribute embedded in a model's representation space that shapes outputs without explicit programming.
- **debiasing** — OpenAI's claimed technique to make DALL·E 2 generate images reflecting population diversity, revealed to be prompt keyword insertion rather than model retraining.
- **foundation model** — A term popularized by Stanford researchers framing large models as the ideological base of contemporary machine learning research.
- **semantic compression** — The learned mapping of visual-world representations to points in a feature space, functioning as a cultural technique.
- **feature space** — The abstract coordinate space into which visual representations are compressed and where concepts like race are dissolved and politically neutralized.
- **unmarked sign** — A literal blank sign appended to prompts to trouble whiteness as the default category of the human.
- **technical modes of whiteness** — The specific operational ways whiteness functions within machine learning systems.
- **machinic concept** — Whiteness reconceptualized and resurrected within the logic of the machine.
